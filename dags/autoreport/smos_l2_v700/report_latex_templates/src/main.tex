    \section{Report summary} \label{sec:summary}

    \noindent This report validates \ac{SMOS} \ac{L2} (v700) surface soil moisture for the period \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Common']['interval_from']>$} to \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Common']['interval_to']>$} against \ac{ISMN} in situ \ac{FRMs}, ERA5-Land reanalysis and \ac{SMAP} \ac{L3}.
    Of the \detokenize{$<len([v for k, v in ReportVars.items() if k.startswith('Tracking ') and 'tracking' in v])>$} tracked metrics, \textbf{\detokenize{$<len([v for k, v in ReportVars.items() if k.startswith('Tracking ') and v.get('tracking', {}).get('tracking_status') == 'ok'])>$}} are within their expected range, \textbf{\detokenize{$<len([v for k, v in ReportVars.items() if k.startswith('Tracking ') and v.get('tracking', {}).get('tracking_status') == 'not ok'])>$}} are outside, and \textbf{\detokenize{$<len([v for k, v in ReportVars.items() if k.startswith('Tracking ') and v.get('tracking', {}).get('tracking_status') == 'none'])>$}} could not be classified (Table \ref{tab:val_run_list}).
    Chapter \ref{sec:tracking} puts the results into the context of the previous reports in this series (summary in Table \ref{tab:tracking_status}), and Chapter \ref{sec:results} contains the detailed results of each validation run.
