\section{Performance tracking} \label{sec:tracking}

\noindent This chapter tracks the agreement of the \ac{C3S} SM COMBINED, ACTIVE and PASSIVE products with \ac{ISMN} in situ measurements and with ERA5-Land (Chapter \ref{sec:datasets}) across the reports in this series.
Each report covers one calendar quarter; consecutive reports therefore do not overlap in time.
For each product, the following metrics are tracked, with different definitions of the \textit{expected range}:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \ac{ISMN}: \textbf{Pearson's R} (only significant correlations, $p \leq$ \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['p_mask_thres']>$}) across all sensors. The expected range is derived from the previous reports, to detect unexpected changes in the temporal agreement with the in situ data.
    \item \ac{ISMN}: The \textbf{\ac{ubRMSD}}, separately for sensors in cropland, grassland and tree cover\footnote{Land cover class (\ac{ESA} \ac{CCI} Land Cover, 2010) of the in situ site as provided in the \ac{ISMN} metadata, aggregated as in the \ac{PQAR}. The classes ``urban areas'' and ``other'' contain only few sensors and are not tracked.}. The expected range is fixed to the \ac{C3S} accuracy requirement of 0--\kpithres{}~\unitsm{}.
    \item ERA5-Land: \textbf{Pearson's R} (only significant correlations) and the \textbf{\ac{ubRMSD}} across all grid points. The expected range is derived from the previous reports. As ERA5-Land is not an error-free reference, the accuracy requirement is not applied here.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Expected range (R, ubRMSD against ERA5-Land):} The medians across all locations from up to \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Common']['n_epochs'] - 1>$} previous reports are used to fit a normal distribution.
The \detokenize{$<format(ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['alpha'] * 100, 'g')>$}\,\% and \detokenize{$<format(100 - ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['alpha'] * 100, 'g')>$}\,\% quantiles of this distribution define the expected range for the median of the current report.
A median outside this range is flagged (\textcolor{red}{red}).
At least \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['min_history']>$} previous reports are required to derive an expected range; otherwise the metric is not classified (\textcolor{gray}{gray}).
Note that the agreement with the reference data varies with the season (e.g. vegetation, frozen soils in winter), which widens the expected range.
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Expected range (ubRMSD):} The \ac{C3S} \ac{KPI} for the accuracy of the COMBINED product (KPI.SM.D3.1) requires a \ac{ubRMSD} of 0.01--\kpithres{}~\unitsm{}, depending on land cover and climate \citep{c3spqar}.
The same threshold is applied to the ACTIVE and PASSIVE products, whose \ac{ubRMSD} is also given in \unitsm{} after the scaling to the in situ data (Chapter \ref{ssec:settings}).
A land cover class is flagged (\textcolor{red}{red}) when the median \ac{ubRMSD} of its sensors exceeds \kpithres{}~\unitsm{}; it is not classified (\textcolor{gray}{gray}) if no sensor of the class has valid results.
For reference, \ac{GCOS} specifies a more ambitious requirement of \gcosthres{}~\unitsm{} for surface soil moisture \citep{gcos2022}.
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Change at common locations:} The median across all sensors also depends on \textit{which} sensors are included, which can change between reports.
Therefore, the median change of the metric at the locations shared with the previous report is given in addition ($\Delta$ prev.).
It is informative only and does not affect the tracking status.

\subsection{Data availability} \label{ssec:tracking_availability}

\noindent Two indicators show whether the amount of data the metrics are based on changes between reports.
They are informative only and are not part of the overall tracking indicator.
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Spatial coverage:} The number of ERA5-Land grid points for which the comparison was successful (status GOOD), per product (Fig.\ \ref{fig:tracking_coverage}).
As ERA5-Land is spatially and temporally complete, a drop indicates that the products provide valid retrievals at fewer locations, e.g.\ due to frozen or snow-covered soils in winter, or a gap in the satellite data. In contrast to the number of \ac{ISMN} sensors, it is not affected by the availability of in situ data.
\detokenize{$<ReportVars['Coverage']['coverage']['summary']>$}
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Number of observations:} The distribution of the number of common time stamps per ERA5-Land grid point with valid results after temporal matching (n\_obs), which is the same for all three products (Fig.\ \ref{fig:tracking_nobs}).
As ERA5-Land is gap-free, n\_obs reflects the temporal availability of the satellite data (temporal reference: \ac{C3S} SM COMBINED).
A drop in n\_obs at an unchanged number of validated grid points indicates a temporal gap in the data stream (e.g.\ an outage that produced empty files): the metrics are then still computed at the same locations, but from fewer observations, and are less robust.
\detokenize{$<ReportVars['Nobs']['tracking']['summary']>$}
