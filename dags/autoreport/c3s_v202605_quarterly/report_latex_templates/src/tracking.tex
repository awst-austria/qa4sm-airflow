\section{Performance tracking} \label{sec:tracking}

\noindent This chapter tracks the agreement of the \ac{C3S} SM COMBINED, ACTIVE and PASSIVE products with \ac{ISMN} in situ measurements (Chapter \ref{sec:datasets}) across the reports in this series.
Each report covers one calendar quarter; consecutive reports therefore do not overlap in time.
For each product, two metrics are tracked, with different definitions of the \textit{expected range}:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Pearson's R} (only significant correlations, $p \leq$ \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['p_mask_thres']>$}) across all sensors. The expected range is derived from the previous reports, to detect unexpected changes in the temporal agreement with the in situ data.
    \item The \textbf{\ac{ubRMSD}}, separately for sensors in cropland, grassland and tree cover\footnote{Land cover class (\ac{ESA} \ac{CCI} Land Cover, 2010) of the in situ site as provided in the \ac{ISMN} metadata, aggregated as in the \ac{PQAR}. The classes ``urban areas'' and ``other'' contain only few sensors and are not tracked.}. The expected range is fixed to the \ac{C3S} accuracy requirement of 0--\kpithres{}~\unitsm{}.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Expected range (R):} The medians across all sensors from up to \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Common']['n_epochs'] - 1>$} previous reports are used to fit a normal distribution.
The \detokenize{$<format(ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['alpha'] * 100, 'g')>$}\,\% and \detokenize{$<format(100 - ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['alpha'] * 100, 'g')>$}\,\% quantiles of this distribution define the expected range for the median of the current report.
A median outside this range is flagged (\textcolor{red}{red}).
At least \detokenize{$<ReportVars['Tracking R_between_0-ISMN_and_1-C3S_combined']['tracking']['min_history']>$} previous reports are required to derive an expected range; otherwise the metric is not classified (\textcolor{gray}{gray}).
Note that the agreement with in situ data varies with the season (e.g. vegetation, frozen soils in winter), which widens the expected range.
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Expected range (ubRMSD):} The \ac{C3S} \ac{KPI} for the accuracy of the COMBINED product (KPI.SM.D3.1) requires a \ac{ubRMSD} of 0.01--\kpithres{}~\unitsm{}, depending on land cover and climate \citep{c3spqar}.
The same threshold is applied to the ACTIVE and PASSIVE products, whose \ac{ubRMSD} is also given in \unitsm{} after the scaling to the in situ data (Chapter \ref{ssec:settings}).
A land cover class is flagged (\textcolor{red}{red}) when the median \ac{ubRMSD} of its sensors exceeds \kpithres{}~\unitsm{}; it is not classified (\textcolor{gray}{gray}) if no sensor of the class has valid results.
For reference, \ac{GCOS} specifies a more ambitious requirement of \gcosthres{}~\unitsm{} for surface soil moisture \citep{gcos2022}.
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Change at common locations:} The median across all sensors also depends on \textit{which} sensors are included, which can change between reports.
Therefore, the median change of the metric at the locations shared with the previous report is given in addition ($\Delta$ prev.).
It is informative only and does not affect the tracking status.

\subsection{Data availability} \label{ssec:tracking_availability}

\noindent Two indicators show whether the amount of data the metrics are based on changes between reports.
They are informative only and are not part of the overall tracking indicator.
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Spatial coverage:} The number of \ac{ISMN} sensors for which the validation was successful (status GOOD), per product (Fig.\ \ref{fig:tracking_coverage}).
A drop indicates that fewer locations could be validated, e.g.\ due to frozen soils in winter, missing in situ data, or missing satellite retrievals.
\detokenize{$<ReportVars['Coverage']['coverage']['summary']>$}
\\[1em]
\noindent\textbf{Number of observations:} The distribution of the number of common time stamps per sensor after temporal matching (n\_obs, Chapter \ref{ssec:results_nobs}), which is the same for all three products (Fig.\ \ref{fig:tracking_nobs}).
A drop in n\_obs at an unchanged number of validated sensors indicates a temporal gap in the data stream (e.g.\ an outage that produced empty files): the metrics are then still computed at the same locations, but from fewer observations, and are less robust.
\detokenize{$<ReportVars['Nobs']['tracking']['summary']>$}
